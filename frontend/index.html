In collaboration with the SEC

SEC Memorandum Circular No. 16, Series of 2025 is the Philippines' sustainability reporting rule.

It requires listed companies to disclose their climate-related risks and greenhouse gas emissions, in phases starting FY2026. EcoNav gives them route-level CO₂ data to report with.

Effective January 2026.

Why it matters

Our mission: emissions that are tracked properly and visible to everyone

Carbon tracking is no longer optional for Philippine companies, and EcoNav gives them one dependable source of route emissions.

EcoNav calculates the CO₂ of every route by distance and vehicle type, so the data is accurate, comparable, and open to regulators and the public.
